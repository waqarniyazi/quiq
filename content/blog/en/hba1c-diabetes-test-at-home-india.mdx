## What Is HbA1c and Why It's Better Than Random Sugar Tests

Unlike a fasting or random blood sugar test, which gives you a snapshot of your glucose level at a single moment, the HbA1c test measures your average blood sugar over the past 2-3 months. It does this by checking the percentage of haemoglobin (a protein in your red blood cells) that has sugar attached to it. This three-month average is far more reliable for understanding long-term glucose control because it isn't affected by what you ate for breakfast or a stressful morning.

This means no more strict overnight fasting or scheduling your day around a lab appointment. You can take the QUIQ HbA1c test at any time of day, from the comfort of your home. It provides a stable metric that doctors worldwide trust for diagnosing and monitoring diabetes, making it an essential tool for proactive health management.

Regular HbA1c monitoring is crucial for several groups. ## How the QUIQ HbA1c Test Works

The process is designed for simplicity and clinical accuracy. Using the provided lancet, you collect a small drop of blood from your fingertip. This drop is placed on the test strip, where it naturally flows forward through capillary action. As it moves, it interacts with specific markers. Within 10 minutes, coloured lines appear on the strip.

You then compare these lines to the provided shade card to read your result. The ranges are clearly defined: below 5.7% is normal, 5.7% to 6.4% indicates pre-diabetes, and 6.5% or higher falls in the diabetic range.
