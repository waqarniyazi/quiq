## What Lateral Flow Technology Can Actually Detect

Lateral flow technology is the brilliant, simple science behind that familiar test strip. Think of it as a mini-lab on a stick. It works by detecting specific proteins, antibodies, or antigens in a tiny sample of your blood, saliva, or urine. When these target molecules are present, they trigger a visual signal—like the appearance of a line. This isn't limited to viruses or hormones. The same core principle can be calibrated to find markers for vitamin deficiencies, thyroid imbalances, cardiac stress, specific infections, and even certain cancers. It’s a versatile platform that brings lab-grade accuracy to your home, delivering results in 10-15 minutes with over 95% clinical accuracy. This means you can get clear, actionable insights without the wait, the travel, or the anxiety of a clinic visit.

## How the Technology Works (A Simple Explainer)

You might wonder how a small kit can deliver such important results. The process is designed for simplicity. Each QUIQ kit contains a sterile lancet for a nearly painless finger-prick, a capillary dropper to collect the blood sample, a test cassette, and a buffer solution. You place a few drops of blood into the sample well, add the buffer, and wait 10-15 minutes. Inside the cassette, your blood travels along the strip. If the target biomarker (like Vitamin D or Troponin) is present in your sample, it binds to specific antibodies, creating a visible line in the test zone. The appearance of this line—compared to a control line—tells you the result. It’s a straightforward, fail-safe process where the complex science happens behind the scenes, leaving you with a clear visual outcome.
