# QUIQ website

quiqhealth.in: product pages, the blog and the research repository.

## Language

**Blog category**:
The single topic a blog post is filed under (e.g. Self-Testing, Policy & Regulation); each has its own page with an intro.
_Avoid_: tag, filter, tab, section

**Category page**:
The page listing every post in one blog category, with that category's intro.
_Avoid_: archive, filter view
